THE COACHING LOOP

Four steps from raw data
to lower scores.

Most launch monitors hand you 14 numbers and walk away. Koda turns them into a coaching conversation you can actually use.

01
Connect

Sync your Garmin R10. Pair in 30 seconds — no extra hardware, no fiddling.

02
Hit

Take your normal swing. Koda streams 14 metrics from the R10 and scores every shot out of 100.

03
Ask

Open the Coach Hub between sets. Ask anything about your session and get real coaching back.

04
Improve

Koda detects trends across your sessions — spotting recurring patterns and gives you actionable advice to correct what's holding you back.
